Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

What Certificate Metadata We Collect

When you upload a certificate for issuance or verification, our system extracts and may store the following metadata:

  • Recipient name (as printed on the certificate)
  • Course or programme title
  • Issuing authority or organisation
  • Certificate issue date
  • Certificate ID or credential number (if present)
  • A SHA-256 cryptographic hash derived from the above fields

We do not collect financial information, government IDs, login credentials, or biometric data.

Why We Collect It

Extracted metadata is used exclusively to:

  • Generate a deterministic cryptographic hash that uniquely identifies the certificate
  • Record that hash on the blockchain ledger so future verification is possible
  • Allow third parties (employers, institutions) to verify a certificate's authenticity via QR code or verification link

Data is never used for marketing, profiling, advertising, or sold to any third party.

Uploaded Files Are Deleted Immediately After Processing

Your original certificate file (image, PDF, or DOCX) is saved to a temporary server location solely for the duration of the extraction process. Once extraction is complete — whether successful or not — the file is permanently deleted from our server. We do not retain, archive, or transmit your original document. Only the extracted metadata listed above is kept.

How Verification Works

When a certificate is issued, its extracted metadata is hashed using SHA-256 and recorded as an immutable block on our local blockchain ledger. When a certificate is later submitted for verification, the same extraction and hashing process is applied and the resulting hash is compared against the ledger. A match confirms the certificate's authenticity.

Verification links and QR codes point to a public URL that displays the verification result for a specific token. These links do not expose personal data beyond what was originally submitted and recorded.

Data Retention

Because the blockchain ledger is append-only and immutable by design, records stored on the chain are retained indefinitely. Database records in our verification index are also retained indefinitely to support future verification requests. If you require removal of your data, please contact us using the details below and we will assess your request.
